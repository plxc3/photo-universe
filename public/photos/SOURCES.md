# Demo photo sources

The 48 demonstration photos are public images from [Lorem Picsum](https://picsum.photos/), which serves photographs from Unsplash. They are stored locally at 400 × 280 pixels so the demo needs no remote image requests after installation.

Photos are used under the [Unsplash license](https://unsplash.com/license). Photographer attribution and original image links are recorded below and in `manifest.json`. These demo images are unrelated to the private photos in the reference video.

## Import and texture limits

- Up to 3,000 accepted local image files per import; empty files and files larger than 40 MiB are skipped.
- The importer creates local blob URLs and does not upload images. It avoids decoding the same full batch twice. Width and height start at zero for local photos and become available after atlas preparation.
- Atlases have 16 columns, 128 px cells, at most 256 successful images, and only the rows they need. Each cell contains a centered 124 × 84 px photo rectangle (x=2, y=22), center cropped with cover fitting. The returned cellRect describes this rectangle for UV calculation.
- `photoIndices` maps each atlas cell to its original index in the input photo list. An atlas cell is one successfully decoded photo; the renderer may repeat those photos across many particles. Particle count is separate from unique photo count.
- Atlas-array `diagnostics` reports `inputCount`, `decodedCount`, `skippedCount`, and skip details. Common image headers are checked before decoding; images above 100 megapixels or 32,768 px on either edge are skipped.
- Each bitmap is resized during decoding, drawn to a cell, and immediately closed. At most four thumbnails are decoded concurrently. JPEG, PNG, GIF, WebP, BMP, ICO, AVIF, SVG, and HEIF headers are recognized; actual format support depends on the browser. Unsupported or damaged files are reported as skipped. TIFF is accepted as an image file but currently skipped during atlas preparation.
- Call `releasePhotos(photos)` once a replaced local photo set is no longer displayed, and dispose the renderer texture objects when replacing atlases.
